Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026

Overview

Heatiz is designed with your privacy in mind. Your data stays on your device and in your personal iCloud account. We do not operate servers, and no personal data is sent to or stored by us.

Location Data

Heatiz requests access to your precise GPS location during match recording on Apple Watch. Location data is used exclusively to build your match heatmap and compute movement statistics. It is stored locally via HealthKit and your iCloud account. Location data is never shared with third parties.

Health & Fitness Data

Heatiz reads workout data, GPS routes, heart rate, distance, and active calories from Apple Health. This data is created by the Apple Watch app during match recording and read by the iPhone app for display. Health data is never transmitted outside of HealthKit and your personal iCloud. Heatiz does not sell, share, or monetise any health or fitness information.

iCloud Sync

Match annotations (goals, assists, ratings, positions, weather snapshots) are stored in CloudKit, which syncs across your personal Apple ID. This data is private to your iCloud account and is not accessible to us.

Weather

Heatiz uses Apple's WeatherKit framework to fetch historical weather conditions for completed matches. The request uses your match location and date; no personal identifier is included. WeatherKit requests are subject to Apple's own privacy policy.
