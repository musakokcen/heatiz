Your match, mapped.

Heatiz turns your Apple Watch into a match tracker. Every run, sprint and position is captured automatically and mapped onto the exact pitch you played on.

Version 1.1 — redesigned for iPhone, iPhone Duo and Apple Watch.

Everything you need to know about your match

Recorded live on your wrist, broken down the moment the final whistle goes.

🗺️

GPS heatmap, automatically

Record via Apple Watch and get a heatmap overlaid on the actual pitch you played on — pitch selector included, so orientation and shape always match.

🎨

Six heatmap styles

Glow, Hex, Zones, Contour, Dots, and a Trail coloured by speed that draws every sprint you made.

🔄

Half-time side switch

Tap once on your Watch at half-time and your heatmap stays aligned with the direction you were attacking.

⌚️

Live on your wrist

Heart rate, distance and sprint count updating live during the match — plus goal and assist logging on the fly.

⚡️

Movement analytics

Max speed, sprints, high-speed running distance, acceleration/deceleration and work rate — with a first vs. second half breakdown.

🏃

Speed zones & sprint map

See how long you spent walking, jogging, running and sprinting, with every sprint effort drawn as a line over your heatmap.

🌦️

Match conditions

Historical weather for the match fetched automatically, plus trimming to exclude warm-up and cool-down from your stats.

📈

Season trends

Every match in one scrollable season view, with charts for distance, goals & assists, rating and positions played.

🔋

Soccer Readiness

A daily readiness score from your sleep, HRV and recent training load tells you whether to push or rest — and how ready you were for every match you've played.

📤

Shareable heatmap cards

Eleven templates in story, post and square shapes. Pick your stats, then post to Stories or save straight to Photos.

☁️

Syncs everywhere

Every match closes your Activity rings and saves to Apple Health, with iCloud sync keeping your season in step across iPhone and Watch.

🔗

Strava, connected

Share matches to Strava as activities with your heatmap attached, and import soccer workouts recorded in other apps.

📱

Made for iPhone Duo

Unfold and Season spreads into a spacious layout you can arrange in Settings.
