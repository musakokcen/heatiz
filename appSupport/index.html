Do my matches show up in Apple Fitness?

Yes. Your Apple Watch saves every match into Apple Health as a soccer workout, so it appears in the Fitness app next to your other activities and counts toward your Move and Exercise rings. Heatiz then reads that same workout back to build your heatmap and stats — nothing is locked inside this app.

Why can't I use the Workout app during a match?

Apple Watch runs only one workout at a time, so while Heatiz is recording, the built-in Workout app cannot start its own session. Everything you would check there is on the match screen instead: swipe to the metrics page for live heart rate, distance, calories, top speed, and sprints. Your match still lands in Apple Health and the Fitness app when it ends.

No matches appearing

Heatiz reads workouts from Apple Health. If no matches appear, Health access may not be granted. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Heatiz and enable all categories.

No heatmap or GPS track

Heatmaps require location access during recording on Apple Watch. If no map appears, location permission may be missing. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Heatiz Watch App and set to 'While Using'.

No heart rate or calorie data

Heart rate and calories are read from Apple Health. If these show '–', grant Health access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Heatiz.

Movement stats are blurred

Movement stats (speed, sprints, HSR) require you to select your pitch first. On the match screen, tap the map card or the map toolbar button to draw the pitch boundary.

iCloud sync not working

Match annotations sync via iCloud. Ensure you are signed in to iCloud on both devices: Settings → [your name] → iCloud, and that iCloud Drive is enabled.

Weather not showing

Historical weather is fetched automatically after a match when a GPS location is available. If it doesn't appear, check your internet connection and try pulling to refresh on the season list.
