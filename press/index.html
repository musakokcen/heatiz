About Heatiz

Heatiz turns your Apple Watch into a match tracker. Every run, sprint and position is captured automatically and mapped onto the exact pitch you played on. It's the fitness app built for soccer — record matches on Apple Watch, replay every run as a GPS heatmap, and track goals, assists, and stats across your whole season.

Short description

Key features

GPS heatmap generated automatically on the actual pitch played on, with a half-time side switch that keeps orientation correct · live heart rate, distance and sprint tracking on the Watch during a match · movement analytics (max speed, sprints, high-speed running, acceleration/deceleration, work rate) with a first vs. second half breakdown · speed zones and a sprint map overlaid on the heatmap · automatic historical weather for each match · season trends across distance, goals, assists, rating and positions played · a daily Soccer Readiness score from sleep, HRV and training load · shareable heatmap cards for Stories and group chats · Apple Health and iCloud sync across iPhone and Watch.
